# GigaSculpt guide

This guide shows you how to sculpt with GigaSculpt, page by page. If you are new, start with
[Getting started](getting-started.md), then read [The interface](interface.md). After that, jump
to whatever you need.

GigaSculpt is young. Everything here is built and works today on Linux. The Windows and macOS
builds are new and less tested; where something works differently there, or not yet, the page
says so.

## Learn the app

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): open the app, make a first sculpt and save it.
- [The interface](interface.md): the window's shelves, trays and menus, moving the view, held
  keys, and changing keys.
- [Keys](keys.md): every built-in key, in one list.
- [Settings](settings.md): the pen, held keys, navigation, sliders, the interface's size and
  theme, files and autosave.

## Sculpt

- [Brushes](brushes.md): every brush, what it does and when to reach for it.
- [Brush settings](brush-settings.md): size, strength, falloff, pen pressure, alphas, stroke
  types, depth, and saving and loading brushes.
- [Masking, visibility and face groups](masking.md): hold parts of the surface still, hide what
  is in the way, and split the surface into groups.
- [Symmetry](symmetry.md): mirror, parallel and radial strokes.
- [Detail and remeshing](detail.md): Live Detail, subdivision levels, Voxel Remesh, Quad Remesh,
  Decimate and Project Detail.
- [Parts, the Gizmo and Deform](parts.md): work with several parts, move and pose them, and
  reshape a whole part at once.
- [Looks and paint](looks.md): MatCaps and materials, Lit mode and its lights, the floor and
  wireframe, and painting color.

## Files and other programs

## A tip that works everywhere

Hold Ctrl with the pointer over any button, switch or slider in the window: a box shows what it
does, the command it runs and its key. Hold Ctrl+Alt too for the command's full description.
